Privacy Policy for MyFx

Last updated: 21 November 2026

MyFx ("the app") is a file manager for Android. This policy explains what the app does with information. Contact: [YOUR PUBLIC SUPPORT EMAIL]

Summary

The developer does not collect, receive or sell any personal data. The app has no account, no ads and no analytics or crash-reporting service. Everything the app stores stays on your device, and the app only talks to servers you set up yourself.

Information stored on your device

  • Connection details you enter (name, host, port, user name, password, key) are saved on your device so you can reconnect. Passwords and keys are encrypted with the Android Keystore.
  • Trusted server certificates and SSH host keys that you confirm are saved on your device, so the app can recognise the server next time.
  • The file server's user name and password, if you set up the file server, are saved on your device.
  • Settings, bookmarks, scheduled syncs, recent places, the recycle bin, thumbnails and caches are stored locally. If you turn on "Keep unfinished transfers", the list of unfinished transfers is stored locally too.
  • The app lock PIN is stored as a salted hash. Fingerprint and face unlock are handled by Android; the app never sees them.
  • Backup files you create are saved where you choose. You can protect them with a passphrase; passwords are included only if you set one. The developer never receives them.
  • Android's own backup is not used. The app is set so that Android does not copy its data to your Google account.

Files

The app reads and changes files only when you ask it to, or when a sync you scheduled runs. It uses the "All files access" permission so that it can manage files and folders anywhere on your storage. File contents and names are not sent to the developer.

Network use

The app connects only to servers you configure (SMB, SFTP, FTP/FTPS, WebDAV) and to addresses you choose to scan on your own Wi-Fi network. Data you transfer goes directly between your device and that server. The developer has no access to it.

  • Network scan: when you use the scan feature, the app checks addresses on your local network for common file-sharing ports. Results stay on your device.
  • File server: if you switch on the built-in file server, the chosen folder is available to devices on your local network until you switch it off. You set the login.
  • Fonts: the app may download its typeface (Montserrat) through Google Play services, which is subject to Google's privacy policy.

Sharing with third parties

The app does not share data with any third party. If you open or share a file with another app, that app's own privacy policy applies to what it receives.

Permissions

  • All files access: to show and manage files anywhere on your storage. This is the purpose of the app.
  • Install packages: only when you choose to install an APK file you opened.
  • Notifications and background services: to show transfer and sync progress and keep them running.
  • Network and Wi-Fi: to reach your servers and scan the local network.
  • Biometric: for the optional app lock.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Security

Logins are encrypted on your device. Data sent over the network is protected only as well as the protocol you choose (SFTP, FTPS and HTTPS are encrypted; plain FTP, HTTP WebDAV and unencrypted SMB are not, and the app tells you when a connection is not encrypted).

Your choices

You can delete any saved connection, clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
