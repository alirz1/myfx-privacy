Privacy policy: Music Pulse

Last updated: 2026-11-23

This policy explains what the Music Pulse Android app ("the app") does with information. The developer is Ali Raza ("I", "me").

Summary

  • The app does not collect data for me. I don't run servers that receive your data, and the app has no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.
  • Your music library, playlists, settings and play history stay on your phone, unless you turn on Android backup or export your settings.
  • When you use features that look things up online, the app sends the song details needed for that lookup to the services listed below.
  • If you connect a NAS, the app talks directly to your NAS. I never see that traffic.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your music library, read from the phone's media storage.
  • Playlists, favorites, play history (counts and last-played times), queue, settings and voice-search corrections.
  • Saved lyrics, search results and cover-art images, kept in the app's storage. These are temporary or cached and are cleaned up by the app.
  • NAS connection details, including the NAS password, saved in the app's settings.

When the app connects to the internet

Only when you use a feature that needs it:

  • MusicBrainz (musicbrainz.org): song, artist and album details for Auto tag.
  • Cover Art Archive (coverartarchive.org): album cover images.
  • Deezer (api.deezer.com): search and album and artist pictures, used when MusicBrainz is busy or has no image.
  • Apple iTunes Search (itunes.apple.com): album cover images.
  • LRCLIB (lrclib.net) and ChartLyrics (api.chartlyrics.com): synced and plain lyrics.
  • Your NAS, over SFTP or SMB, if you turn it on.

The requests include the title, artist and album of the song being looked up. They also include the app's name and version in the request header. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Android backup

Music Pulse uses Android's automatic backup for settings, playlists, favorites, play history and voice corrections. Android sends this backup to your Google account, where Google encrypts it in transit and at rest. The backup does not include saved lyrics, caches, or the reject list. You can turn off backup in your phone's settings.

Permissions and why the app asks for them

  • Read music (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on older Android): to show your library.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): only for voice search. The app does not store or send the audio itself. The words are turned into text by Android's speech recognition service, which may be provided by Google on your phone.
  • All files access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): to write tags and lyrics into your song files, and to delete or move rejected songs on the phone when you choose that mode. You can say no and the app keeps working with reduced features.
  • Notifications: to show playback controls.
  • Internet and network state: for the lookups and NAS connection listed above.
  • Foreground service (media playback): to keep music playing with the screen off.

Children

Music Pulse is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Sharing

I do not sell or share personal information. The app shares song details only with the lookup services above, when you use those features.

Your choices

  • Turn off lyrics saving, Auto tag, NAS copying or backup in the app's settings or your phone's settings.
  • Remove the app to delete everything it stores on your phone.
  • Delete files on your NAS yourself, at any time.

Security

Passwords you enter for the NAS are kept in the app's private storage. Connections to the NAS use SFTP or SMB.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new "Last updated" date.
